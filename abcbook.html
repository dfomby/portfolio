ABC Book

[2026]
Layout Design, Cover Design

The "ABC book" is a collection of important design and typography principles that are playfully presented in book form. Each page is dedicated to one word of the alphabet/design-related term. A vibrant palette of blue and pink is balanced with cool beige tones to create strong contrast and visual harmony while maintaining readability. The combination of decorative and understated typefaces adds character to each layout while ensuring consistency, clarity, and legibility across headings and body text.
