    About Me

    My name is Dvash, and I have an Associate of Science degree in Multimedia Programming & Design, at Borough of Manhattan Community College. I will be continuing my education majoring in Computer Information Systems at Baruch College, for a Bachelor of Business Administration degree.

    As a born and bread New Yorker, I often find my inspiration in the city's eclectic visual language. I enjoy utilizing my illustrative, technical and design skills to create full-fledged projects. Not only have I always liked experimenting with my creativity, seeing the results form together in the end is what always drives me to design. Ultimately, I aim to be a Multimedia Designer who bridges the gap between technology and design through the impact of my creations.

    If I'm not designing, I'm always looking for a new craft to experience or a new book to read, especially in the fantasy genre!
