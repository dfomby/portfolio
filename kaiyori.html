Kaiyori

[2026]
Brand Design, Logo Design, Menu Design, Advertising Design

Kaiyori is a hypothetical Japanese restaurant that sells Kaiseki meals. Kaiyori aims to advertise their hospitality and their traditional cooking for their Artisanal dishes.

In making this brand design, the main purpose reflected in the design is the creation of a brand that fits in either a traditional or modern aesthetic for the restaurant. This is to properly portray Kaiyori's traditional values, while adhering to modern appeal. The brand identity conveys the restaurant's bold and resonating design elements, while portraying comfort and friendliness, adhering to the restaurant's purpose.

Ideation

The brand's purpose:
To effectively portray the authenticity of traditional Kaiseki meals in a modern setting

The brand's personality:
Vibrant, Bold, Friendly, Inviting

Potential consumers:
❖ Consumers who are looking for a unique Japanese dining experience
❖ Consumers seeking a friendly, modern Japanese restaurant
❖ Consumers curious about 'Kaiseki' and seeking traditional Japanese meals

Researching 'Kaiseki'
❖ Dynamic seasonal menu
❖ Multi-course experience
❖ Wholehearted hospitality
❖ Uses traditional cooking techniques to enhance high-quality ingredients, and highlights natural flavor

Restaurant Consumer Trends:
❖ Restaurant atmosphere & dining experience
❖ Being able to try new cuisines; Artisanal dishes
❖ Growing demand for healthier options on menus
❖ Viral trends; Foods that look great to post on social media
❖ Life-style content (healthy & enjoyable lifestyle)
❖ Casual Dining (High-Quality foods, Less formal setting visually)

The Restaurant needs a brand that conveys friendliness and adheres to modern appeal, while conveying the traditional values and importance of Kaiseki.

Working Drafts

Drafting the Logo

Intitial Sketches

For the logo, I tried initially sketching a more playful take on the logo to be ‘friendly’, but I realized that I wanted a more traditional approach to the logo design, while still being modern to match the brand's personality.

Final Sketch

Eventually, I was able to create a sketch that appears modern yet traditional, and still looks friendly to potential customers. The logo is a rice plant, or 'Oryza sativa', ectruding out of a soup bowl with the lid on the side, to convey the art of traditional cooking.

Food Menu Design

Food Menu Sketch

For the cover of the food menu, I wanted to convey the hospitality of Kaiyori through the sketch I created of a chef serving a dish.

Food Menu Cover Draft

For my first draft of the menu, I realized that it was greatly lacking color and striking elements to make it memorable and bold. I also wanted to properly incorporate the logo in a way that it stands out.

Food Menu Cover Final Draft

The final draft of the menu properly conveys the title, with striking bold elements to make it a unique menu cover. It also conveys the cohesive color palette of the brand, with the addition of portraying the restaurant's hospitality.
