Kaiyori

Contemporary Kaiseki Restaurant Identity

Kaiyori is a hypothetical contemporary Japanese restaurant identity, inspired by Kaiseki cuisines. The brand identity explores how traditional Japanese culinary values are adaptable into a modern and friendly visual system.

My Role: Graphic Designer

Year: 2026

Tools: Illustrator, Photoshop, Procreate

Project: Concept/Hypothetical Project

Final Designs

The Challenge

Rooted in Japanese hospitality, Kaiseki refers to a traditional multi-course dinner that emphasizes seasonal ingredients and artistic presentation with warm hospitality.

In a contemporary lens, the portrayal of Kaiseki may feel inaccessible to younger audiences who are curious, but unfamiliar with Kaiseki cuisine. The main challenge is to create a brand identity for Kaiyori that respects and represents the traditions of Kaiseki, while adhering to modern appeal.

The problem I need to solve: How can traditional Kaiseki be communicated in a friendly and appealing branding identity, while being suitable for contemporary audiences?

Research and Ideation

The brand's purpose:
To effectively portray the authenticity of traditional Kaiseki meals in a modern setting

Key Insights

Insight 1: Traditional Menu

Kaiseki emphasizes a dynamic seasonal menu. It uses traditional cooking techniques to enhance high-quality ingredients, and highlights natural flavor.

Insight 2: Hospitality

Kaiseki Cuisines are entered around wholehearted hospitality and a multi-course experience.

Restaurant Consumer Trends:
❖ Restaurant atmosphere & dining experience
❖ Being able to try new cuisines; Artisanal dishes
❖ Growing demand for healthier options on menus
❖ Viral trends; Foods that look great to post on social media
❖ Life-style content (healthy & enjoyable lifestyle)
❖ Casual Dining (High-Quality foods, Less formal setting visually)

Target Audience

Professionals and culturally curious diners looking for a unique, authentic and modern dining experience, who are overall unfamiliar with Kaiseki cuisines.

Concept Development

Logo Design

Intitial Sketches

To start conceptualizing the brand identity, I started sketching out different logo designs for Kaiyori. For the logo, I tried initially sketching a more playful take on the logo to be ‘friendly’, but I realized that I wanted a more traditional approach to the logo design, while still being modern to match the brand's personality.

Final Logo Sketch

Eventually, I was able to create a sketch that appears modern yet traditional, and still looks friendly to potential customers. The logo is a rice plant, or 'Oryza sativa', ectruding out of a soup bowl with the lid on the side, to convey the art of traditional cooking.

The components of the logo:

Oryza Sativa -> Represents Japanese culinary tradition

Bowl & Lid-> Portrays the dining experience of Kaiseki, along with the soup bowls that are often uses for Kaiseki meals

Circular Elements -> The logo uses rounded and circular elements to convey a sense of friendliness, representing Kaiyori's warm hospitality

Menu Drafts

After developing the direction of the brand through the logo, I began applying them to the menu to properly understand how the identity would appear beyond the logo design. This helped me understand how to properly apply the brand identity across the different mediums I wanted to portray.
