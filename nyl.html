New Youth Leaders

[2025-2026]
Flyer Design, Advertising Design

New Youth Leaders is a nonprofit organization that provides primarily after school programs to students in grades K-12. The goal of NYL is to provide career-connected activities for students to identify their passion at an early age, and learn important career readiness skills needed to enter the workforce. NYL has also collaborated with another program, Life of Hope, for their afterschool programs.

For my graphic design internship at NYL, I've completed flyers and graphics that were used to promote New Youth Leaders' programs, and to convey relative important information for families who have children in the program.
