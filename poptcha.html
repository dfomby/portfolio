Poptcha

Matcha brand Identity

Poptcha is a hypothetical newly established matcha brand, primarily known for its bottled matcha drinks. The brand identity explores the balance of portraying the vibrancy of Poptcha with the combination of capturing matcha's beneficial and calming appeal.

My Role: Graphic Designer

Year: 2025

Tools: Illustrator, Photoshop, Procreate

Project: Concept/Hypothetical Project

Final Designs

The Challenge

Matcha is a finely ground powder made from Japanese green tea leaves. It contains multiple benefits such as caffeine and L-theanine.

Matcha today has been popularized for its benefits and calming appeal. Poptcha's identity wants to maintain the appeal of matcha, while also portraying a vibrant image to stand out from competitors. The main challenge is to create a brand identity for Poptcha that appeals to its consumers while standing out as a matcha brand.

The problem I need to solve: How can Poptcha's identity portray vibrancy while appealing to matcha consumers?

Research and Ideation

The brand's purpose:
The brand needs to advertise the benefits of their matcha, and adhere to the aesthetics that contribute to the trend of matcha.

Key Insights

Insight 1: Matcha Benefits

Matcha contains caffeine and L-theanine (amino acid that promotes alertness and focus without causing jitters; good substitute for coffee). Matcha is also rich in antioxidants and other nutrients

Insight 2: Matcha Trend

The trend surrounding matcha portrays around its 'aesthetic', or its visually pleasing color palettes. Matcha is also trending for its tasty indulgence, while being a healthier alternative to coffee.

Target Audience

Matcha consumers who want the convenience of ordering different and unique flavors of matcha online, while still being able to consume the benefits of matcha.

Concept Development

Drafting the Logo

Intitial Logo Art Sketches

For the logo art, I tried sketcing various iterations that represented the brand's name, and ideation of the fun exploding flavors of their matcha products. It was a long run of trial and error, as I bounced around from putting too much and too little detail.

Final Logo Art Sketch

Eventually, I was able to create a sketch that I thought had a good balance of fun, while still symbolizing matcha. The logo represents a bottle with matcha exploding out of it, with the top drawn as a matcha whisk, sent flying. It signifies a different way of conveying that the matcha is exploding in flavor.

The components of the logo:

Matcha Whisk -> Represents matcha as a whole to be easily recognizable for daily matcha consumers

Bottle-> Portrays the bottled matcha that Poptcha sells

Playful matcha explosion -> Signifies the matcha exploding in flavor, and conveys the friendliness of the brand

The logo overall is purposefully drawn to be lighthearted and less 'corporate' to establish trust for the targeted audience.

Main Logo draft

To incorporate the logo art with the chosen typeface, I created different iterations of what the main logo should look like. I also experimented with two different typefaces within the logo.

Final Logo Draft

With the final logo design, I adjusted the design of the 'o' in poptcha from my second iteration, to have the subtle appearance of an exclamation mark. The final design allows the logo name and design to be able to stand out on their own, while still looking cohesive together. The design overall has eye catching elements and colors that easily stand out, and portray the brand's ideation of fun flavored matcha products. The logo is also easily adaptable to represent different flavors of the matcha products by changing its secondary colors.
