Poptcha

[2025]
Logo Design, Advertising Design

Poptcha is a hypothetical newly established matcha brand selling matcha products, primarily bottled matcha drinks.

The brand design aims to capture matcha’s appeal: a drink that is fun, colorful, and yet provides a calming experience. This brand identity captures matcha’s balance of energy and calm through a playful yet sophisticated visual system. Designed to stand out among competitors, the branding features vibrant graphics, bold packaging, and a dynamic logo treatment that attracts attention while remaining approachable. A palette of calming greens contrasted with vivid purples creates a strong visual presence, while elegant display typefaces paired with modern body fonts maintain clarity, balance, and cohesion throughout the brand experience.

The Process

Ideation

The brand's purpose:
To effectively advertise their matcha products to potential consumers

Color Scheme:
Vibrant, Fun, and stands out

Potential users/consumers:
❖ Consumers who want the convenience of drinking matcha already made in a bottle
❖ People looking for different and unique flavors of matcha, while still consuming the benefits of matcha

Researching the benefits of matcha:
❖ Contains caffeine and L-theanine (amino acid that promotes alertness and focus without causing jitters; good substitute for coffee)
❖ Rich in antioxidants and other nutrients

The 'trend' of matcha:
❖ Visually vibrant pleasing color
❖ Indulgence while also being healthy

Important considerations of matcha:
❖ High-quality matcha powders can be too expensive
❖ Matcha is often mixed with added ingredients to improve the taste, leading to a high calorie and sugar intake

The logo should stand out in comparison to other matcha logos; The brand would need to successfully advertise the benefits of their matcha, and adhere to the aesthetics that contribute to the trend of matcha.

Drafting the Logo

Intitial Logo Art Sketches

For the logo art, I tried sketcing various iterations that represented the brand's name, and ideation of the fun exploding flavors of their matcha products. It was a long run of trial and error, as I bounced around from putting too much and too little detail.

Final Logo Art Sketch

Eventually, I was able to create a sketch that I thought had a good balance of fun, while still symbolizing matcha. The logo represents a bottle with matcha exploding out of it, with the top drawn as a matcha whisk, sent flying. It signifies a different way of conveying that the matcha is exploding in flavor.

Main Logo draft

To incorporate the logo art with the chosen typeface, I created different iterations of what the main logo should look like. I also experimented with two different typefaces within the logo.

Final Logo Draft

With the final logo design, I adjusted the design of the 'o' in poptcha from my second iteration, to have the subtle appearance of an exclamation mark. The final design allows the logo name and design to be able to stand out on their own, while still looking cohesive together. The design overall has eye catching elements and colors that easily stand out, and portray the brand's ideation of fun flavored matcha products. The logo is also easily adaptable to represent different flavors of the matcha products by changing the secondary colors.
